# X publishing checklist

- Post in sequence 01 → 25.
- Attach exactly 3 media assets to each post.
- Keep the first image as the strongest hook.
- Keep Unicode styling readable; never over-style the entire post.
- Do not claim demo metrics are live.
- Do not imply simulated trades are executed.
- Reply to strong engagement with product details rather than repeating the same CTA.
- Pin the strongest introduction post once the thread has momentum.
