- Attach exactly 3 images to each post, in order (interface → agent → feature).

- Keep Unicode styling readable — headers are styled, body stays plain.

- Reply to engagement with product detail rather than repeating the CTA.
- Pin the strongest intro post (01, 03, or 11) once the thread has momentum.
